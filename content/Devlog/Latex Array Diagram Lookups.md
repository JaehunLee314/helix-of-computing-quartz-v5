## Square Diagrams

- Single Square
$$
\begin{array}{ccc} 
& \text{P} & \\ 
X & \stackrel{a}{\longrightarrow} & Y \\ 
\llap{\scriptstyle b}\bigg\downarrow & & \bigg\downarrow\rlap{\scriptstyle \ c} \\
R & \stackrel{d}{\longrightarrow} & Z
\end{array}
$$

- Arrow & Square
$$
\begin{array}{cccc} 
\text{P} \qquad & & \text{Q} & \\ 
U \qquad & X & \stackrel{a}{\longrightarrow} & Y \\ 
\bigg\downarrow\rlap{\scriptstyle \ c} \qquad & \llap{\scriptstyle b}\bigg\downarrow & & \bigg\downarrow\rlap{\scriptstyle \ c} \\
W \qquad & R & \stackrel{d}{\longrightarrow} &D
\end{array}
$$
- 3 by 2 Square
$$
\begin{array}{ccccc} 
& & \text{P} & & \\ 
A & \stackrel{a}{\longrightarrow} & B & \stackrel{b}{\longrightarrow} & C \\ 
\llap{\scriptstyle c}\bigg\downarrow & & \llap{\scriptstyle d}\bigg\downarrow & & \bigg\downarrow\rlap{\scriptstyle \ e} \\
D & \stackrel{f}{\longrightarrow} & E & \stackrel{g}{\longrightarrow} & F
\end{array}
$$

- Single Square with Diagonal
$$
\begin{array}{ccc} 
& \text{P} & \\ 
X & \stackrel{a}{\longrightarrow} & Y \\ 
\llap{\scriptstyle b}\bigg\downarrow & \stackrel{e}{\searrow} & \bigg\downarrow\rlap{\scriptstyle \ c} \\
R & \stackrel{d}{\longrightarrow} & Z
\end{array}
$$
